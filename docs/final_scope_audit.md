The following remain limits even if every runnable check passes:

- Six requested journal sources lack recovered full methods. Their verified abstracts, partial text or metadata support only the explicitly described claims; inaccessible text is unresolved prior-art overlap, not evidence of novelty. `docs/literature_audit.md` identifies each source and the successful author-paper sources.
- The F750 manufacturer describes `Log(1/Reflectance)` but does not explicitly expose the logarithm base or firmware arithmetic. The decadic emulator interpretation is conventional and documented. Flat detector/reference response, digitized curves and measured spectral resolution limit claims about physical LEDs.
- Seasons 4 and 5 were already used in an earlier local study. This run seals them from fitting and selection, but they are reused external benchmarks within the project lineage, not previously untouched prospective validation.
- No physical scanner has been built or measured. The next experiment must pair adaptive and fixed scans on the same local mangoes, use an oven-dry reference, measure emitted/received response and noise, energy per scan, acquisition time and placement repeatability, and reserve a genuinely new season or orchard for transfer evaluation. No field accuracy, energy saving, economic benefit or patentability conclusion follows from replay.
- Author names/affiliations, contributions, funding, conflicts and institution-specific declarations need author input. No repository release, submission, purchase, outreach or patent filing is part of this run.

Final verification can establish computational completeness while the scientific result is negative or inconclusive. It cannot replace these missing physical and independent-validation inputs.
