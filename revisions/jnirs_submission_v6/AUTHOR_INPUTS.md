4. Any additional acknowledgements, with the acknowledged individuals' agreement where appropriate.

7. Confirm the rights to included material, originality, publication/submission status and all-author approval before using the draft cover letter. Confirm any applicable institutional requirements.

## AI policy requires substantive review

The Acknowledgements and draft cover letter disclose Codex assistance with code, literature checking, analysis/reporting assistance and drafting. This is more extensive than spelling or grammar assistance.

Sage's current policy distinguishes disclosed uses from prohibited uses and includes the wording **“Generation of data or submission using a series of prompts.”** Its application to this extensively assisted workflow is not resolved by adding a disclosure. The authors need to assess the actual workflow against the policy and obtain editorial clarification if needed before deciding to submit. This package does not certify eligibility or completed human review. No mango observations were fabricated; the empirical data and simulated-channel assumptions remain explicitly identified.

Sources: [Sage AI policy](https://www.sagepub.com/journals/publication-ethics-policies/artificial-intelligence-policy), [JNIRS author instructions](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/jns).
