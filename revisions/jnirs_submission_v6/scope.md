# Dataset-only paper scope

The user requests a complete paper and current work based entirely on the existing dataset, with no external collection or physical experiment required for completion. This revision consolidates completed analyses without refitting models, reselecting on held outcomes, or treating candidate-v4 tuning scores as independent accuracy.

Empirical material: the official Mendeley version-3 mango scans and already documented spectral-response simulation. Seasons 4 and 5 are held-out seasons within the same dataset, not a requirement for a new external dataset. Published emitter curves remain explicit simulation assumptions; they are not new measured factors or field data.

Paper aims: quantify prediction versus simulated reading count, test the proposed stopping objective against matched alternatives, diagnose predictor and acquisition-order effects, and document where conclusions do and do not hold. The paper may conclude no demonstrated adaptive advantage. Harvest timing is motivation only because observed labels are dry matter, not validated pick/wait decisions or postharvest outcomes.

The original analysis and exploratory follow-ups must remain distinguishable. Reused benchmarks and repeated development-data use must be disclosed. All supported numerical claims must trace to saved predictions or verified result files. Missing full methods of some cited papers limits detailed prior-art comparison, but no unsupported originality claim is required for this empirical paper.

Completion means a full manuscript, supplement, numerical evidence, reusable sources, compiled and visually reviewed PDFs, preservation/integrity records and a final verification report. Field validation, harvest thresholds, hardware building, new fruit collection and physical energy/timing measurements are outside this paper scope. Optional future work is not a completion blocker. Author names and declarations may remain author inputs; no submission is authorized.
