# Evidence available for the research paper

The paper can examine when simulated extra spectral measurements improve mango
dry-matter estimates and why an adaptive method may fail to add value. It should
retain the negative findings, credible fixed-acquisition controls, optical
assumptions and the history of repeated development-data use.

The completed studies and this preparation serve different purposes:

| Artifact | Evidence it supplies | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Original study | Adaptive replay, external-season comparisons and prototype screen | Proposed objective benefit was inconclusive; achieved budgets differed; prior local use was disclosed |
| Methodology v2 | Controlled prediction-family/subset and stopping-objective comparisons | Development-only exploratory diagnosis; no promoted adaptive policy |
| Fixed-design v3 | Exact-four-reading primary-bank PLS improvements and fixed-six engineering sensitivity | Fold-specific selections, conditional intervals, no universal six-channel device |
| Candidate v4 | One documented selection rule, one frozen six-channel model, two fitted comparators and a prospective validation plan | A prepared research artifact, not a new independent performance result |

The final candidate uses 720,760,820,890,910,940 nm. Its selection statistics are
available in results/selection_scores.csv only as tuning evidence. Do not reuse
the earlier fold-specific six-channel RMSEPs as the accuracy of this final model.
Future results must come from new, independently documented observations.

No new learning algorithm, established novelty, validated picking recommendation,
or physical hardware saving follows from this freeze. Exhaustive subset
selection and PLS are established methods. The publication contribution must be
judged against the full methods of close optical-selection and acquisition
precedents; missing journal full texts remain listed in ../../docs/literature_audit.md.

Harvest timing is the intended application. A prediction-versus-laboratory study
can establish dry-matter accuracy in its sampled setting. Agreement with a
separately justified maturity rule is another endpoint. A claim of better picking
decisions or fruit quality requires a prospective field comparison and the
relevant outcomes. These are distinct claims and should be written separately.

The existing manuscript is preserved as the original study's artifact. This
note and the v2/v3/v4 reports are evidence for a later author revision; no revised
manuscript or submission is claimed here.
